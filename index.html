GET STARTED

Set up your business

Your business name and currency are stored locally on this device. Connect a Google backend when the secure provisioning service is configured.

Google connection safety

Never enter your Google password here. The production Google sign-in and automatic spreadsheet / Apps Script provisioning flow requires a verified Google OAuth client and deployment configuration. This starter does not pretend that provisioning is already active.

Mwakete Marketplace connection

The Mwakete adapter is a documented integration boundary. It must be connected to verified existing Mwakete authentication and APIs before marketplace accounts or live inventory are enabled.
