WHOLESALE

Price lists

Create lists such as Trade or Distributor. Set each product's price for a list in Products; products without a price for a list use their base price. Assign a list to a trade customer in Customers, or pick one at the till. Saved on this device only.

WHOLESALE

Accounts and invoices

Credit ledger: not production-ready

Your Apps Script has no verified sign-in yet. Do not use the ledger with real customer or payment data until authenticated Google sign-in is available. The ledger stays off until you set LEDGER_ENABLED=true in your Apps Script project properties.

0 record(s) rejected by your ledger

They are kept on this device and are not synchronised. The reason is shown on each record. Fix the cause (for example, turn the ledger on), then retry.

Customer balances

Balance = invoices on account minus payments received. Confirmed is the last balance your ledger returned; pending is what this device has recorded since. Both can be out of date while offline.
