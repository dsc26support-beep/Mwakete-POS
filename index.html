INVENTORY

Products

Pack sizes (optional)

A pack sells several units at once, e.g. Carton = 24. Leave the pack price empty to charge unit price × units. Stock is always counted in single units.

Import or export products (CSV file)

Use a file saved from Excel or Google Sheets as CSV. The first row must be headings such as name, price, stock, barcode. Nothing is saved until you press Import, and existing stock counts are kept unless you choose otherwise.

STOCK ALERTS

Low stock

Products at or below their reorder level. Stock counts are kept on this device.

TRANSACTIONS

RECEIPTS

Delivery queue

Receipts waiting to be sent by SMS, WhatsApp or email. Sending opens your own app; you confirm it was sent.

CREDIT NOTES

Refunds and returns

Open a sale and press Refund…. A refund never changes the original sale; it is recorded as a credit note. Credit notes reach your cloud account when you are signed in.

CASH DRAWER

Register

Count the cash in the drawer when you start and when you finish. The app works out what should be there from this device's cash sales, payments and refunds. Selling is never blocked when the register is closed.

Customers

WHOLESALE

Price lists

Create lists such as Trade or Distributor. Set each product's price for a list in Products; products without a price for a list use their base price. Assign a list to a trade customer in Customers, or pick one at the till. Saved on this device only.

WHOLESALE

Accounts and invoices

Credit ledger: not production-ready

Your Apps Script has no verified sign-in yet. Do not use the ledger with real customer or payment data until authenticated Google sign-in is available. The ledger stays off until you set LEDGER_ENABLED=true in your Apps Script project properties.

0 record(s) rejected by your ledger

They are kept on this device and are not synchronised. The reason is shown on each record. Fix the cause (for example, turn the ledger on), then retry.

Customer balances

Balance = invoices on account minus payments received. Confirmed is the last balance your ledger returned; pending is what this device has recorded since. Both can be out of date while offline.

PAYMENTS

Record a payment received

Cash received against a trade customer's account. It reduces their balance. Not confirmed until you sync.

    SECURITY

    This device and screen lock

    These protections live on this device. They stop casual misuse and make changes to the activity log detectable. They cannot stop someone who controls the device itself, so keep backups and use cloud sign-in for staff.

    Device code: … · App version · Storage kept by the browser: unknown

    Manager approval

    Ask for a manager's PIN when a discount or a refund is bigger than a limit you set. Leave a limit at 0 for no limit. Signed-in owners and managers are not asked.

    Activity log

    BUSINESS TYPE

    Choose your POS

    Pick what fits your business. You can change this later; your products and sales are kept.

    WHOLESALE

    Tax

    Used on Wholesale invoices and quotes (coming next). Prices are entered before tax. Default 12.5%.

    YOUR DATA

    Backup and support

    Customers, price lists and settings live only on this device. Download a backup file now and then, and keep it somewhere safe (for example your email or a USB stick). Restoring puts those records back; it never deletes anything, and a record with the same ID is replaced.

    Diagnostics for support

    If something is not working, copy this and send it to support. It has version numbers, counts and error messages only: no sales, product or customer names, amounts or email addresses. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
